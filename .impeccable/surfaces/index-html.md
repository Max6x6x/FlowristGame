# Surface brief: index.html (whole app)

Scope: single-page study app — Gyakorlás (practice, with optional 50-question exam round), Lista, Admin. Mode: Operate.

Audience/job: one florist student, phone and PC equally; sees a plant photo, types Latin and/or official Hungarian name, reveals, learns.

Constraints: no name may be visible before reveal (alt text, credit, file names neutral); hints are progressive (1st: letter count per word, then one more letter per press); zoom on the photo; desktop = two columns (photo left, inputs right); phone = single column, photo on top.

Decisions from the user: Warm consumer app direction chosen over Field Guide / Herbarium / Standard. Practice and exam merged into one screen. Mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/1t9wW6zPJHw6zR1XLgBs5D (C · Warm app).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A friendly pocket coach for one exam: soft cards, a thumb-reachable bottom bar, one saturated green only on what can be pressed. Refuses the grey form-with-a-header quiz look.

OWN-WORLD: warm tinted ground #f3efe8, white cards, radii 14/24/28, action green #2f8f4e reserved for actionable elements and correct state, amber #9a5b00 for "almost", red for wrong; Nunito (rounded humanist) at comfortable sizes; spring press feedback.

STORY: she opens it, sees a photo, types, taps "Mutasd!", sees exactly which letter was off and the official names, taps Következő. Wrong ones come back sooner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: phone — mode segmented control top, photo card ~40% height with zoom button, two rounded inputs under it with hint lines, Súgó + Mutasd! row, bottom tab bar (Gyakorlás/Lista/Admin). Desktop — top nav, photo 7fr left, answer card 5fr right.

FORM: Warm consumer app surface (dealt challenger, competitive), seed key d9c232cc.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
